In-trade check-ins

How often the app asks you to look up while a position is open. It only nudges while the app is on screen — the bar in the header turns amber, nothing buzzes. Set these to whatever actually suits your holding times; 10 and 15 are only a starting point.

Put 0 in either box to turn that nudge off. The Check in button stays available whenever you want it.
